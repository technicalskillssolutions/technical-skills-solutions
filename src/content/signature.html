EMAIL SIGNATURE STUDIO

Gmail Email Signature Maker

Create a handwritten-style signature image and a professional email footer that you can add to Gmail.

1. Create your signature image

This creates a visual signature image for the footer of your emails. It is processed locally in your browser. For a personal handwritten signature, upload a clear photo or scan of your signature; the typed option is an accessible alternative.

Use a close, well-lit image with dark ink on plain white paper. White-background removal is approximate; inspect the exported image before using it.

Use a mouse, stylus or touchscreen to draw. This canvas is pointer-based; screen-reader users can use the typed-name or uploaded-image method instead.

Signature image preview

No signature has been generated yet.

Gmail’s recommended signature image size is about 300–400 px wide and 70–100 px high. This tool exports a 400 × 100 px PNG. The image is an email footer graphic, not proof of identity or a certificate-based digital signature.

2. Add your contact details

These details remain selectable text when you paste them into Gmail. Keeping contact information as text makes the email footer more accessible than placing every detail inside one image.

Your email contact details will appear here.

Fill in the details you want to show at the end of your emails.

3. Add the image to Gmail

  1. Download the PNG signature image above.
  2. On a computer, open Gmail and choose Settings, then See all settings.
  3. Under General, find Signature and create a new signature.
  4. Enter or paste your contact details. Put the cursor where the signature image should appear.
  5. Choose Insert image, select Upload, and select the downloaded PNG file.
  6. Choose the displayed image size, set defaults for new emails and replies if required, then Save Changes.

Gmail Help: Create a Gmail signature on a computer. Google recommends approximately 70–100 px high by 300–400 px wide; image maximum is 100 px high by 1,000 px wide.

Android note: Gmail’s Android app has a separate Mobile signature setting that accepts text. If you do not create a separate mobile signature, Gmail says new messages use the signature created on your computer. See Gmail signature on Android.
