PRIVACY & DATA HANDLING

Privacy Policy

Understand what is processed locally, what is sent to an external provider and how to use the tools carefully.

Last reviewed: 6 November 2026

1. Information you provide

We do not ask for an account to use the local browser utilities. If you contact the team by email, phone or a community platform, the information you choose to share is handled by that communication provider.

2. Local browser processing

Password generation, SIP calculations, text transformations, QR creation, typed/drawn/uploaded Gmail signature image rendering, contact-text formatting and the OCR workflow are designed to process their working input in your browser. The Gmail signature image and contact footer are rendered locally in your browser; uploaded signature images are not intentionally sent to a server. The OCR feature runs in your browser and does not intentionally upload the selected image or PDF file to a server. The File Integrity & Hash Checker uses the browser's Web Crypto API to calculate SHA-256, SHA-384 or SHA-512 locally; selected file contents are not uploaded. The OCR engine may use cached language assets from its configured distribution host. OCR page results are saved incrementally in this browser's IndexedDB so they can be restored after a reload; use the tool's Clear Results control to remove them.

3. External services

  • URL shortening: When you request a short link, the destination URL is sent to an external link-shortening service through its public API. The provider processes that destination URL under its own terms and privacy policy. QR codes are generated locally in your browser. Technical Skills Solutions does not store the destination URL, returned short link or click analytics. Do not shorten password-reset links, private tokens, account invitations or other sensitive URLs.
  • Hosting and model assets: GitHub Pages or other configured hosting providers serve the site files. Tesseract language data and browser libraries may be downloaded from their configured distribution hosts.
  • Community and store links: Opening YouTube, Telegram, WhatsApp, Microsoft Store or other external destinations is subject to that provider's own privacy policy.

4. Cookies and local storage

The site uses browser local storage for preferences such as theme, high-contrast mode and text size in 2-pixel increments. OCR page text is stored in IndexedDB. User-selected files themselves are not intentionally uploaded by the OCR tool. These values are stored on your device and can be removed using your browser's site-data controls.

5. Security

Do not paste secrets into public tools. Check external links before opening them and keep your browser and operating system updated.

6. Accessibility

We aim to support keyboard navigation, NVDA, TalkBack, clear labels, large touch targets, visible focus, high contrast and status announcements. Different browsers and assistive-technology versions can behave differently, so real-world testing remains ongoing.

7. Children and third-party websites

The site is not designed to collect personal data from children. External services linked from this site operate independently and have their own terms and policies.

8. Policy changes and contact

This policy may change when tools or providers change. For questions, use the Contact page.
