ABOUT THE PROJECT

About Our Mission

Technology Without Barriers — engineering useful tools and software with accessibility at the centre.

Who We Are

Technical Skills Solutions is a technology initiative founded by Ayush Pareek with a clear purpose: to build powerful, practical and accessible digital tools. The project focuses on web utilities, Our software and technical resources that work for blind, low-vision and sighted users.

Our work is guided by a simple principle: accessibility should be part of the product from the beginning. Keyboard operation, meaningful labels, predictable focus and screen-reader feedback are requirements—not optional finishing touches.

Government recognition and registration

Technical Skills Solutions is associated with the MSME registration details supplied for this initiative.

  • Administrative ministry: Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises, Government of India
  • Udyam registration number: UDYAM-RJ-30-0147268
  • National Industry Classification: 85499 — Educational Services & Technical Training
  • Enterprise classification: Micro Enterprise (Services)

Our Vision

To make high-performance software and accessible technology work together, and to demonstrate that a complete digital experience can serve users with different ways of interacting with a computer.

Our Core Mission

  • Accessibility first: Provide clean, intuitive controls that work with screen readers and keyboard input.
  • High-performance engineering: Build dependable utilities with sensible resource use and transparent behavior.
  • Practical empowerment: Create tools that support education, productivity, communication and independence.
  • Privacy and security: Explain where data is processed and avoid unnecessary collection.

What We Value

  • Innovation: Improve algorithms and workflows through testing and feedback.
  • Universal design: Make controls, output and errors understandable to assistive technology.
  • Integrity: Be clear about limitations, third-party services and product status.

Founder's Statement

“True innovation is achieved when software operates without friction for every user. Our mission is to eliminate digital barriers and create technology that gives people greater control, confidence and independence.”
— Ayush Pareek, Founder

Our Commitment

We are committed to continuously improving usability, privacy, documentation and accessibility based on practical testing and user feedback.
