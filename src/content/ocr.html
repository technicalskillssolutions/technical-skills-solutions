Document OCR & Extractor

Extract text from images and scanned PDFs with multilingual recognition, page-by-page progress and controlled memory use.

Extract text from images and PDFs

Choose one or more image or PDF files. Each PDF may contain up to 1,000 pages. Every page is handled one at a time inside short batches, so the browser does not keep 1,000 rendered pages in memory together.

Privacy: Your selected image and PDF files are processed in this browser and are not uploaded by this OCR tool. The recognition work runs locally in your browser. The OCR engine, PDF.js worker and selected language data are fetched from their package/CDN distribution on first use and may be cached by your browser. Your selected document is not uploaded to our server. Detailed engine and language-asset loading messages are intentionally hidden.

PDF limit: 1,000 pages per file. Maximum file size: 300 MB each. Large scans may take substantial time; use 10-page batches for long PDFs.
